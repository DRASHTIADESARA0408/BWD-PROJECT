The Spiritof Érice

Authenticity is the new luxury

Érice was founded on a simple premise:

Jewelry should not be a costume. It should be an extension of the self.

We moved away from fast fashion trends to focus on heavy gold work, substantial settings, and designs that feel permanent.

We are a slow-fashion house. We produce in small batches to ensure waste is minimized and quality remains our obsession.

The Ethics
Behind the Gold

Modern technology meets old-world hands. While we utilize 3D formulation to ensure perfect ergonomics, every stone is set by hand in our atelier.

Our diamonds are conflict-free, and our gold is 100% recycled, certified by the Responsible Jewellery Council.

Elara VanceCo-Founder

Elara Vance is the artistic visionary behind Érice, infusing each piece with her deep-seated passion for sculptural forms and understated elegance. With a background in fine art and a meticulous eye for detail, Elara believes jewelry should be an intimate extension of the self, not merely an adornment.

Her dedication to timeless design ensures every creation is quietly extraordinary and crafted to be cherished for generations, embracing the brand's slow-fashion ethos and commitment to enduring beauty.

Julian ThorneCo-Founder

Julian Thorne brings the conscience and innovative spirit to Érice. Driven by a commitment to sustainability and ethical practices, Julian spearheaded the brand's dedication to using 100% recycled gold and conflict-free diamonds.

He champions the unique blend of modern technology and old-world hands, ensuring precision complements the artisanal skill of hand-setting each stone.

Julian's vision ensures Érice remains a pioneer in responsible luxury, proving that exquisite craftsmanship and ethical production can coexist harmoniously.
